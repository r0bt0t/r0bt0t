Hey, I'm Rob 👋

I'm a Broadband and Connectivity Engineer with six years of field experience troubleshooting residential broadband, in-home Wi-Fi and network connectivity issues.

I'm currently building on that experience by developing my wider networking knowledge, working towards the Cisco CCNA and documenting my practical learning through GitHub.

🌐 What I'm working on

I completed the Summer of CCNA programme through NetworkChuck Academy, with training from instructors including Jeremy Cioara, Chuck Keith, Zach Hill and guest lecturers including Jeremy from Jeremy's IT Lab.

I'm continuing my CCNA studies through additional course material while spending a lot of time building, troubleshooting and documenting practical networking labs.

My aim is to develop beyond simply knowing the commands and build a genuine understanding of why networks behave the way they do, how to troubleshoot them, and how technical changes affect real users and engineers.
